# Privacy Policy — ZenGrid Sudoku

*Last updated: September 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **ZenGrid Sudoku** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
ZenGrid Sudoku is built as an offline-first puzzle game:
- We do **not** require account creation or personal login credentials.
- We do **not** collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- We do **not** access your contacts, microphone, or personal files.
- When you use the puzzle scanner feature, images are processed **100% locally on your device** in memory and are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All game progress, including:
- Solved and active puzzle states
- Move replay history
- Win streaks and best times
- App preferences (dark mode, timer, mistake mode)

are stored strictly **locally on your device** (`user://` application directory). Uninstalling the app or clearing application data removes this local data.

## 3. Advertising
ZenGrid Sudoku integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements:
- Advertisements adhere strictly to Google Play Policies.
- Ads may use anonymized advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads based on your device settings and regional consent choices (GDPR/CCPA/UMP).
- For details on Google's data practices, visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. Children's Privacy
ZenGrid Sudoku does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
