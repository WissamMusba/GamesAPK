# Play Store Graphic Assets Checklist & Guidelines

Required graphic assets for the Google Play Store listing.

---

## 1. App Icon
- **File**: `icon.png` (Included in this folder)
- **Dimensions**: $512 \times 512$ px
- **Format**: 32-bit PNG (with alpha)
- **Max File Size**: 1024 KB

---

## 2. Feature Graphic
- **Dimensions**: $1024 \times 500$ px
- **Format**: JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- **Max File Size**: 15 MB
- **Prompt provided in `ASSET_PROMPTS.md`**.

---

## 3. Phone Screenshots (Minimum 4, Recommended 5–8)
- **Dimensions**: $1080 \times 1920$ px (16:9 portrait) or $1080 \times 2400$ px (20:9 portrait)
- **Format**: JPEG or 24-bit PNG
- **Recommended 5 Screens**:
  1. `Screenshot 1`: Main Board with pencil notes & color marks. (Header: *"PURE LOGIC, CALM DESIGN"*)
  2. `Screenshot 2`: Educational Technique Hint Modal. (Header: *"LEARN AS YOU PLAY"*)
  3. `Screenshot 3`: Instant Sudoku Camera & Image Scanner. (Header: *"SCAN ANY SUDOKU"*)
  4. `Screenshot 4`: Solve-Back Replay Player. (Header: *"STEP-BY-STEP REPLAY"*)
  5. `Screenshot 5`: Main Menu with 5 Difficulties & Daily Challenges. (Header: *"EASY TO MASTER"*)
