## 2. Reviewer Instructions

- **No login or password required.**
- All 5 difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master), Daily Challenges, Sudoku Scanner, Hint Engine, and Replay System are immediately unlocked and playable offline upon first launch.
- Offline play is 100% functional without an active network connection.
